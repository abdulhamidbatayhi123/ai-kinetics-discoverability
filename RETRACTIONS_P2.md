# Corrections (Paper 2)

Every defect in Paper 2's design, code or text found after it was written, why it was wrong,
and what fixed it. The entries below were first recorded in Paper 1's ledger
(`RETRACTIONS.md` in github.com/abdulhamidbatayhi123/ai-mof-cof-dynamics, Zenodo
doi:10.5281/zenodo.23323685), while both papers shared one repository. They are copied here
verbatim with their original numbers; new Paper 2 entries continue from B85. Commit IDs inside
the copied entries are the original ones: `COMMIT_MAP.tsv` maps each to its commit here.

B76 to B85 were caught before any grid result existed, so none reached a reported result. B86 to B88 were found while the grid ran or after its first verdicts; each is corrected by a dated amendment or a code fix, and the as-run results are kept and reported beside the corrected ones.

| **B76** | *Our own error: a bug recorded as a hardware limit.* Paper 2's M7 inclusion gate (`p2_pilot_m7.py`, PREREG_P2 §3) wrote **"M7 NOT RUN: infeasible on this machine"**. Every seed had failed with the same exception -- PySR accepts only alphanumeric variable names, and the wrapper passed Lib-B's names (`c*q`, `q^2`) straight through. Seed 0's 635 s was Julia compiling before the error; seeds 1-2 'ran' in 0 s. The first re-run exposed a second configuration error of the same kind: `deterministic=True` with multithreading, which PySR also refuses (and the gate, started before its decision rule was fixed, again wrote 'infeasible'). | **Fixed before the freeze; the decision withdrawn.** `p2/pysr_method.safe_names` passes placeholders and maps them back (test added); the gate is re-run unchanged and its outcome recorded in PREREG_P2 §11. No grid result existed. | *Found 2026-10-11 by noticing that two of three seeds took zero seconds.* The gate's verdict rule counted an exception as a failure to meet the limits, so a software error and an infeasible method produced the same words. **A gate must distinguish 'it could not run' from 'it ran and failed'.** |
| **B79** | *Our own error: a cost stated as a range the log does not show.* PREREG_P2 (§4.2, §6, §7 and pre-freeze revision 4: "corrected to the logged 3–7 s per solve") and the Stage-1 draft said the 81 ground-truth solves took **3–7 s each**. `p2_generate.log` shows **1–414 s, median 4 s**; 18 of the 81 took longer than 7 s (the slowest are Langmuir at Da ≥ 316 with Pe × 0.2: 332 s and 414 s), and 25 took under 3 s. | **B82** | *Our own errors: Paper 2's verdict code did not do what its pre-registration said, before and after amendment A1.* Found by a second internal Stage-1 review of the amended plan. (i) **Error rows were dropped**: `_by_da` and the H2a loop skipped rows carrying `"error"`, while PREREG_P2 §4.3 and the Stage-1 text say an error "is recorded as a failure, never dropped". | **B86** | *A column solve that could run for days, stalling the grid.* O3 identifiability made no progress on row 198 of 216 (`step_L1_pe1_da31.6|0.005|0.02|2`) for six days; two O2 rows of the same cell had taken 39 h and 42 h. Cause, reproduced: the measured isotherm at ε > 0 evaluates its error function at c/c_in clipped to [0, 1]; at large trial k the stiff solver crosses that kink at about 5 % of its evaluations and the BDF steps collapse (k = 0.056: 14 s at ε = 0, 13 s at ε = 0.02 without the clip, unfinished after 300 s with it). Fixed by PREREG_P2 amendment A5: a 600 s wall cap per solve in the O2/O3 profile only, a capped solve scored as a failed solve (−∞, as solver errors already were), the capped k values and their adjacency to the interval recorded per row. The clip, the observation model and every finished row are unchanged; O2/O3 identifiability carries no verdict. Tests: `tests/test_solve_cap.py`. | *Found 2026-10-29* when the grid showed no progress after a restart; root cause by timing each k of the stuck row. |
| **B87** | *Our own error: the O1 identifiability profile searched a k range that excluded three cells of the design, and the positive control caught it.* `profile_interval_o1` searched k over 10⁻⁵–10, a range taken from a statement true for the Langmuir column only; the step cells at Da = 0.1 have k = 3.2 × 10⁻⁶. Their likelihood peak was cut at the range's edge, every interval sat at 3.105 k, and the step positive control failed (identifiable 0 of 135), which withheld the step isotherm from every verdict. The stored fields obey the LDF law exactly and the likelihood peaks at the true k (interval [1.0034, 1.0038] k with a range that contains it). Fixed by PREREG_P2 amendment A6: range 10⁻⁷–10 at the same density, an error instead of a silent edge interval, all 51,840 O1 identifiability rows recomputed, the analysis rerun unchanged; the as-run files and verdicts are kept and reported beside the corrected ones. Tests: `tests/test_profile_range.py`. | *Found 2026-11-01*, by diagnosing the failed step positive control: per-probe LDF check, likelihood at fixed k, then the profile's grid. |
| **B88** | *Our own error: M1 discarded its best parameters when a column solve failed during training, against PREREG_P2 A4 ("the best parameters seen are kept").* In 4 of the 9 M1 demonstration fits (Langmuir, Pe×1, Da = 31.6, 100, 316, 1000) a solve of the differentiable column exhausted its step budget (diffrax `max_steps` = 200,000) after one to two hours of training.
